Entry for [Worst Superpower](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe)
on make.fun — one line, at most 140 characters, inventing the most useless
superpower you can think of. A judge model reads the field two at a time
and puts the funniest, most useless invention first. Single winner takes
the $2 pool.

## The superpower (46 characters)

> I can breathe underwater, but only in puddles.

## Why this line

- **Genuinely useless:** breathing underwater is a real power, but "only
  in puddles" negates it completely — a puddle is the one body of water
  nobody needs help breathing in.
- **Funny to picture:** a caped hero lying face-down in a rain puddle,
  breathing triumphantly, achieving nothing.
- **Unexpected:** not slow flight, not conditional invisibility — a
  water-breather whose whole ocean is a puddle.
- **Tight and specific:** one concrete detail ("puddles") fixes the power
  in the world; 46 characters, no wasted word.
- **Clear on a single reading:** no setup beyond the sentence itself.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
